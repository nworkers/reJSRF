### 바뀐 것

* **렌더링 배율.** 모든 렌더 타깃을 게스트 크기의 1~8배로 그린다. 게임(D3D)에는 게스트 크기가 그대로 보이고, GL 텍스처·깊이 버퍼·뷰포트·시저·blit에만 배율이 곱해진다([설계 i080](../design/20261017-i080-render-scale.md)).
  * `REXBOX_RENDER_SCALE=<1~8>`: 처음 배율(기본 1).
  * 창에서 Ctrl+1~Ctrl+8: 실행 중 변경. 프레임 사이에서 적용되며, 색 타깃은 내용을 이어 간다.
  * 데칼의 깊이 바이어스(폴리곤 오프셋 기울기)도 배율에 맞춘다.
* **덤프.** 프레임 덤프와 F9 캡처 BMP는 배율이 곱해진 크기로, owner 맵은 게스트 크기로 저장된다.

## reJSRF v0.0.35 (English)

Changes since **v0.0.34** (issue #80). **The internal rendering resolution can be raised 1x to 8x, also while running.**

Resolved issue: #80, internal rendering resolution scale (1x to 8x) changeable at run time, commit `9e5156f`.

Changes:
* **Render scale:** every render target is drawn at 1x to 8x its guest size. The title (D3D) still sees guest sizes; only GL textures, depth buffers, viewports, scissors and blits are scaled ([design i080](../design/20261017-i080-render-scale.md)).
  * `REXBOX_RENDER_SCALE=<1..8>`: the initial scale (default 1).
  * Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8 in the window: change it while running. It applies between frames, and color targets keep their contents.
  * Decal depth bias (the polygon-offset slope) follows the scale.
* **Dumps:** frame dumps and F9 capture BMPs are written at the scaled size, owner maps at the guest size.

Verified:
* Win32 x86 Debug and Release: zero build warnings; D3D8 probe 163/163; d3d8 check 14,018 cases with 0 differences; decomp check 129 cases with 0 differences.
* Behavior: at scale 1, demo frames 1,500, 3,000 and 4,650 are identical to v0.0.34; scale 2, 4 and 8 dumps have the right sizes and pictures; in the user's play, changing the scale while running works.
