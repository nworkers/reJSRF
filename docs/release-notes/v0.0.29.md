### 바뀐 것

* **광원·재질 상태.** `SetLight`·`LightEnable`·`SetMaterial`이 광원(`D3DLIGHT8`)과 재질을 저장한다.
* **조명식.** 고정 기능 정점 셰이더가 D3D 정점 조명을 카메라 공간에서 계산한다.
  * 방출광, 전역 주변광과 광원별 주변광
  * 방향·점·스폿 광원의 확산광, `SPECULARENABLE`이면 정반사광
  * 재질 색 원천: 정점 형식에 없는 색은 재질로 바꾼다.
* **데모.** 데모의 조명 결과는 흰색으로 잘려 화면은 그대로다. 명암은 v0.0.28의 법선 램프가 맡는다.

## reJSRF v0.0.29 (English)

Changes since **v0.0.28** (issue #66). **Fixed-function lighting is implemented, and the default demo window reports no unimplemented D3D8 path.**

Resolved issue: #66, D3D8 fixed-function lighting, commit `464c215`.

Changes:
* **Light and material state:** `SetLight`, `LightEnable` and `SetMaterial` keep the lights (`D3DLIGHT8`) and the material.
* **Lighting equation:** the fixed-function vertex shader computes D3D's per-vertex lighting in camera space.
  * Emissive, global ambient and per-light ambient
  * Diffuse from directional, point and spot lights, plus specular under `SPECULARENABLE`
  * Material color sources: a color the vertex format lacks falls back to the material.
* **Demo:** its lit result clamps to white, so the picture is unchanged. Shading comes from v0.0.28's normal ramp.

Verified:
* Win32 x86 Debug and Release: zero build warnings.
  * Probes: kernel 56/56, D3D8 158/158, DSOUND 48/48.
  * d3d8 check: 14,018 cases with 0 differences. decomp check: 6/6. HLE pop check: 0 mismatches.
* Behavior: no unimplemented reports in the demo window; no shader errors; not slower than the previous build.
* CI on the pull request.

Known issue:
* Character outlines are thinner than expected, and they were reported to look white on some setups (under investigation).
