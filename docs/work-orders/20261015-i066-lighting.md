# Work order: fixed-function lighting (issue #66)

Design: [20261015-i066](../design/20261015-i066-lighting.md)

1. `d3d8_state`: `SetLight`, `LightEnable` and `SetMaterial` keep the lights and the material.
2. Confirm the game's lights, material, render states and vertex blending with a temporary trace, not committed.
3. `d3d8_ff_shader`: the key (light types, color sources, local viewer) and the lighting GLSL.
4. `d3d8_gl_programs`: uniform locations. `d3d8_draw`: building the key; the light and material uniforms, in camera space.
5. Checks in `rexbox_d3d8_probe`.
6. Demo picture and reports; CPU measured alternately with the previous build; Debug and Release, regression tools.
7. Documents: ARCHITECTURE, the kb, boot-frontier, the guide (probe count), the work log.
